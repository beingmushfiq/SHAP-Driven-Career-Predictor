About Career Predictor

Bridging the gap between human potential and artificial intelligence.

The Technology

Our platform is built upon an XGBoost machine learning model trained on extensive datasets linking academic performance, behavioral traits, and soft skills to successful career outcomes.

However, accurate prediction is only half the battle. We utilize SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), a game-theoretic approach to explain the output of our machine learning models. This ensures absolute transparency—you will always know exactly why a career was recommended to you.

Data Integrity & Privacy

We process your data securely in real-time. Our inference engine is designed as a highly optimized, thread-safe singleton, ensuring your assessment is completely isolated and lightning-fast.
